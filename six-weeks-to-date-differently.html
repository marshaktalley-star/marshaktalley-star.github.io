More Than a Card Game

Experience the connection.

Date Differently™ was created as part of Six Weeks to Date Differently, an intentional dating experience designed to give people more than a few minutes to discover who someone is.

The cards invite you to play, notice, communicate, make decisions, reveal something about yourself and experience how the person opposite you actually interacts with you.

Important: Date Differently™ is an educational, personal-development and social dating activity. It does not provide psychotherapy, psychological treatment or professional matchmaking guarantees. Participants remain responsible for their own dating decisions, personal boundaries and safety.
