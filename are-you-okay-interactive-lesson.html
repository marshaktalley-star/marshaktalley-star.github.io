Interactive Film Lesson

Are You Okay?

A guided learning experience about bullying, cyberbullying, empathy, bystander responsibility, emotional wellbeing, and reaching out for support.

1. Watch the film

Watch closely. Notice what happens at school, what continues online, how Raquelle and Noah respond, and how the film uses visual symbolism to show emotional distress.

Content note: The film addresses bullying, cyberbullying, social isolation, depression, and emotional crisis. Students should be reminded that they may speak privately with a trusted adult or qualified support professional.

2. Learning goals

Recognise bullying and cyberbullying.
Explain how bullying can affect mental health.
Distinguish passive bystanding from safe allyship.
Identify trusted and responsible sources of support.

3. Guided pause points

Pause at these moments during a class viewing. The scene descriptions are used instead of fixed timestamps so the activity works across different uploads or playback speeds.

Pause 1

After Raquelle notices Noah being bullied

What clues show that this is more than harmless teasing? What choices are available to a witness?

Pause 2

When the harassment continues online

How does technology increase the reach, audience, or permanence of the harm? What evidence could be saved before reporting it?

Pause 3

When Raquelle becomes a target

Why might bystanders now become even more reluctant to act? How could a group respond more safely than one student acting alone?

Pause 4

Near the emotional turning point

What warning signs suggest that someone may be overwhelmed? Why must serious concerns be shared with a trusted adult rather than kept secret?

4. Film comprehension and application quiz

Complete all 15 questions. Questions 11–15 ask you to apply the film’s lessons to new situations.
