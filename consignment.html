Before we begin

A few useful answers.

How do wholesale and consignment differ?

Wholesale means purchasing stock upfront. Consignment means holding agreed stock and accounting for sales on an agreed schedule. The written agreement will set out commission, payments, stock responsibility and returns.

Can my store propose its own consignment rate?

Yes. Include the percentage your store retains, payment schedule and any fees in the enquiry form. Terms must be agreed by both parties before products are supplied.

Is there a minimum order?

Send the products and quantities you have in mind. Any minimum quantities will be confirmed with your quotation.

Are these physical books?

Yes. The three books listed here are print editions. This retailer collection does not include ebooks or digital downloads.

How are delivery and unsold stock handled?

Delivery or collection is arranged with each store. For consignment, the stock period, condition of returned items, responsibility for loss or damage and return arrangements are agreed before delivery.

Does completing the form place an order?

No. The form prepares a WhatsApp enquiry for you to review and send. Marsha will discuss availability and terms with you before any order or consignment placement is confirmed.
