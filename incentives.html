Parent Acknowledgement

Students can earn recognition for discussing their homework with a parent or guardian and receiving acknowledgement through WhatsApp, email, a signature, a phone call, verbal confirmation, or another convenient method.

No particular method is required. Students who cannot obtain acknowledgement at home should have an equivalent opportunity to demonstrate their learning.

3. Classroom Birthday Calendar

Birthdays are saved in this browser only. Use first names and enter birthdays only with appropriate permission. Students may opt out of public recognition.

7. Quick Guide for Teachers

  1. Choose two or three incentives your students value.
  2. Explain how students can earn recognition.
  3. Recognise effort, progress and consistency each week.
  4. Encourage parent acknowledgement through any convenient method.
  5. Review homework completion and adjust incentives as needed.

Keep rewards manageable. Students with additional learning needs should have fair opportunities to earn recognition through individual progress and reasonable adjustments.
