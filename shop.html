Why Silhouette’s Café Exists

Wellness was part of my life before it became a brand.

Silhouette’s Café began years ago as my wellness brand in Texas. I sold essential oils, herbal products, skincare, diffusers, jewellery and other pieces connected to the way I thought about healing and everyday wellbeing.

The idea is coming home to Jamaica differently. I still want the physical café one day - plants, natural light, good drinks, movement, conversation and things that help people feel more connected to themselves. This online shop is the part I can bring forward now.

I am not interested in selling wellness as perfection. Life gets messy. The point is to have things around you that help you create the kind of atmosphere you want to live in.

Peace is something we have to protect on purpose.
