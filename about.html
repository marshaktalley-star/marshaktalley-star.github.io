About Marsha

I have never been just one thing.

I am an educator. An author. A creator. A mother. A woman who has built things, lost things, started over and still found another way.

I was born in Kingston, Jamaica, left the island at fourteen, and spent much of my adult life building a life and career abroad. Education became the longest thread in my professional life, but it was never the whole story.

I have taught, led, written books, built businesses, hosted conversations, created programmes and followed ideas simply because I could see what they might become.

That is probably the simplest way to explain me. I see possibility.

A little more of the story

My life has not exactly followed a neat little plan.

I have spent more than two decades in education across the United States and United Kingdom, working with children, young people and adults with different learning, behaviour and support needs. I learned a lot about teaching. I learned even more about people.

Then life kept adding chapters. I became an author, entrepreneur, podcast host, facilitator and consultant. Some things grew from opportunity. Others grew because life backed me into a corner and I had to create another way.

Turning Pain Into Possibility™ is not a cute tagline I found for a website. I have had to live it.

Now I am back in Jamaica, still creating, still learning, still asking questions, and still refusing to believe that what happened before gets to decide what is possible next.

These days

I am building a life that has room for all of me.

I still care deeply about education, behaviour, leadership and the conditions that help people thrive. I also care about writing, creativity, style, wellbeing, good conversations, building useful things and living a life that actually feels like mine.

That is why this website has more than one lane. It is not because I cannot decide who I am. It is because I already know.

I teach.Education is still one of the strongest threads running through my life.
I create.Books, programmes, ideas, conversations, businesses. I like making something from what I can see in my head.
I question things.Especially the things everybody has decided are simply “the way it is.”
I live inspired.Not perfectly. Intentionally. There is a difference.

If something about my story, my work or the way I see the world brought you here, have a look around. You will probably find another side of me somewhere.
