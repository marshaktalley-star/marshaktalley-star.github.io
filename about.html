About Marsha

Live Inspired | Turning Pain Into Possibility™

EDUCATOR · AUTHOR · CONSULTANT

A life sculpted by education, possibility and creating another way.

Born in Jamaica's capital, Kingston, I had my fair share of experiences that would later prepare me to lead in places that sometimes sought to limit me.

My journey has rarely followed a straight line. From education to writing books, entrepreneurship to consultancy, I have found ways to forge through hardship and, at times, create something out of seemingly nothing.

Education has been the longest thread running through my professional life. For more than two decades, I have worked across the United States and United Kingdom as an educator, teacher educator and leader, working with children, young people and adults with diverse learning needs. Those experiences taught me as much about people, systems and leadership as they did about pedagogy.

Beyond the classroom, I became an author, entrepreneur, podcast host, facilitator and consultant. Each chapter expanded how I understood the relationship between people, their environments and their ability to thrive.

Today, those experiences come together in my work with educators, leaders and organisations. I bring the perspective of someone who has worked inside complex systems, led through challenge, built ideas from the ground up and continually found ways to turn difficult experiences into possibilities.

Glimpse of the Journey

Early Years and Education

Education became one of my greatest teachers. Teaching students with diverse academic, behaviour and other needs gave me a close-up view of what helps people flourish beyond societal expectations and limitations. I also learned firsthand what happens when systems make it unnecessarily difficult for them to thrive.

Finding Another Way

Some of my most important professional thinking emerged from difficult periods in my own life and career. They forced me to question conventional ideas about resilience, leadership and wellbeing and to think more deeply about the environments we create for people.

Building Silhouette's Café

Entrepreneurship gave me another classroom. I founded Silhouette's Café as a wellness brand centred on relaxation, natural living and intentional care. Building something of my own strengthened my understanding of vision, creativity, risk and what it means to turn an idea into something tangible.

Becoming an Author

Writing gave me a different way to teach. The Decision Is Just the Beginning and Healing from Emotional Traumas grew from experiences and lessons I believed deserved to be shared beyond a classroom or workshop.

Expanding the Classroom

Over time, my classroom became larger. Teacher development, leadership, professional learning, speaking, podcasting and consultancy allowed me to work with people and organisations in new ways while remaining grounded in the principles that have shaped my career.

Returning to Jamaica

Returning to the country where my story began represents another chapter. I bring home more than two decades of international education experience alongside everything I have learned through leadership, entrepreneurship, writing and life.

The thread connecting it all is simple: I believe people can create possibilities even when circumstances suggest otherwise.
