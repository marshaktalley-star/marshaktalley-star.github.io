4. Email preparation

Enter the address you want to use and the teacher's address. Your email app controls the actual sender; select the matching account in its From menu. No email service or Google Apps Script is required.

After reviewing, choose Print / Save as PDF, save the report, then select Approve & Open Email. Attach the saved PDF to the draft yourself. Email drafts cannot automatically attach files through a normal web page.
