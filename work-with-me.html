People-centred performance

High performance should not require people to break themselves.

Burnout prevention remains part of my work, with the bigger picture in mind: what helps people learn, contribute, perform and stay engaged over time?

I help organisations think about the workplace conditions that affect performance, including leadership style, culture, communication, workload and whether people feel supported.
