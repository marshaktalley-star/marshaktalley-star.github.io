A two-day practical learning experience delivered for the Social Development Commission at The Moneague College in St. Ann, Jamaica.

The programme moved participants beyond simply hearing about technology. They explored how digital tools can be used to create, communicate, build businesses and solve practical problems. Sessions included Canva, AI, website creation, content development and digital entrepreneurship, with participants learning through hands-on activities and real-time creation.
