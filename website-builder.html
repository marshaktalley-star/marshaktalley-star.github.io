Website Development

Your Website Questionnaire

A focused questionnaire for your seven-page solar, engineering and electrical website.

You tell me. I build it. Just give me accurate information about your business. I'll use what you provide to determine how the website should communicate your services.

Brief answers are welcome. Required fields are marked *. If you are unsure about an optional question, leave it blank.

Start here

Business Details & Style

Short answers or bullet points are enough. You do not need to write finished website content.

Write “same as above” if appropriate. Include business hours and any public address.
If you do not have these yet, leave blank.
Include colours, preferred style, anything to avoid, and up to two websites you like with a brief reason. Send your logo in the upload section below.

Page 01

Home

Introduce your business and give customers a clear reason to contact you.

Include where you are based and the towns or parishes you cover.
Include your experience, relevant qualifications or licences, and what makes your service different.

Page 02

Solar Solutions

Help customers understand which solutions you can provide.

Which services do you offer?

Select only what you currently provide.

Explain what is included and the customer problems you solve. Add other services or important exclusions here.
Give the main steps and typical timescale. Include your installation/workmanship warranty and aftercare.

Page 03

Engineering & Electrical

Show the full range of your technical skills.

Include custom technical work customers may not know you provide.
For example, the Leaf adaptor: what was the problem, what did you create, and how did it help? This can also be used on the Projects page.

Page 04

Projects

Real examples help customers understand your work.

For each: general location, customer problem, your solution, equipment used and result. You can refer to your engineering example above without repeating it.
Paste the feedback and the name or initials you have permission to display. Send matching project media below.

Page 05

Solar Information

Give customers simple, practical information before they enquire.

List up to five with brief answers. Include panels, batteries, inverters, system size and what works during a power cut.
Include realistic savings, backup power, maintenance and anything important before buying.
If yes, include the currency, price or range, what it covers and any conditions. Otherwise write “quote only”.

Page 06

Equipment

Help customers understand the components you trust and supply.

List key brands/models, what each is suitable for, manufacturer warranties and any product/specification links.

Page 07

Get an Estimate

These answers will determine the enquiry form I build for your customers.

Consider contact details, location, service, property type, electricity bill/usage, appliances to power, existing equipment, photographs, budget and preferred timing. Tell me which are required and which can wait.
Are site assessments free or paid? When are they needed, and how soon can customers expect a response?
Leave blank to use the email you entered above.

Finish

Files & Final Details

You can also send files or voice notes using the WhatsApp button below.

Combined attachment limit: 10 MB. Use the link field for videos or larger collections.
Paste shareable links and identify the project each belongs to. Make sure I can open them.

Only send photographs, videos and testimonials you have permission to publish.

Your information and uploaded files will be used to understand your business and develop your website. Submitting this questionnaire does not commit you to a project.
