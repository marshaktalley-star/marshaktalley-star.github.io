Website Development

Website Project Questionnaire

Tell me about your business, services and how you work. I'll use your answers to plan the content and functionality your website actually needs.

You don't need to write website copy. Just give me accurate information about your business. I'll use what you provide to determine how the website should communicate your services.

If you don't know the answer to something, leave it blank unless the question is marked as required.

Section 01

About You & Your Business

The essentials I need to introduce you and your business accurately.

List the towns, parishes or wider areas where you normally accept work.
Include solar, electrical, engineering and any other relevant professional qualifications. Include the awarding body where possible.

Section 02

What You Do

I need the full range of your work so the website represents your capabilities accurately.

Include residential and commercial work where applicable: panels, batteries, inverters, backup systems, assessments, installation, maintenance, upgrades and anything else you offer.
Include work that is not strictly solar.

Section 03

How a Project Works

This helps me explain your process clearly to potential customers before they contact you.

For example: initial conversation, site visit, assessment, quote, sourcing equipment, installation, testing and handover.
If it varies, explain what usually affects the timeline.

Section 04

Equipment & Suppliers

For example: local suppliers, distributors, direct imports, ordered specifically for each job, or equipment you keep in stock.

Section 05

Pricing, Warranties & Aftercare

What factors affect the final price?

Section 06

Previous Projects

I want the website to show real work rather than rely only on descriptions of your services.

For each one, briefly tell me what the customer needed, the problem you were solving, what you installed or engineered, and the result.

Project Photos & Videos

Upload photographs and short videos of completed work that could be featured on your website.

Upload limit: Keep the combined size of all attached files at 10 MB or less. For larger videos or a larger collection of photographs, use the link field below.

Paste a shareable Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud, YouTube or other media link if the files are too large to attach.

Section 07

What Customers Need to Understand

I'll use this information to develop useful solar education content for the website.

Section 08

Get an Estimate

Your answer here will determine what I build into the customer estimate form on your website.

Think about everything you normally have to ask before you can advise someone properly — property information, electricity usage, existing equipment, location, photographs, budget, appliances, site conditions or anything else.

Section 09

Business Identity & Contact

Paste the links here.

Section 10

Anything I've Missed?
